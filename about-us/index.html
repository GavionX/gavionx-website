About GavionX

A Hyderabad-based telematics company building devices for people who run vehicles for a living.

Who we are

Helping fleet owners stay on track

GavionX Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a Hyderabad-based telematics company. Our devices cover the three things fleet owners most want to know about a vehicle: where it is, how much fuel it has, and what happened on the road.

Simple

OBD II models plug into the vehicle's port in seconds, with no wiring, no tools and no workshop visit.

Complete

Location, fuel and video from one supplier, so the devices on a vehicle are chosen to work together.

Flexible

Fit a handful of vehicles or a whole fleet, with a plug-in tracker or a hard-wired unit fitted out of sight.
