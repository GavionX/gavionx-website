GX-505

Compact LTE OBD II GNSS tracker with an on-board microphone.

OBD II · Audio

A compact plug-in tracker with audio

The GX-505 is a small OBD II tracker that plugs in and starts working. It reports location over 4G LTE, analyses basic driving behaviour and has an on-board microphone for remote listening.

  • Plug and play
  • Compact size
  • 4G LTE connectivity
  • Multi-source positioning
  • Driving behaviour analysis (basic)
  • Remote listening
  • 9–36 V operating voltage
  • Multiple alerts
