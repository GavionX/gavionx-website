GX-502

LTE OBD II GNSS tracker with on-board diagnostics and driving behaviour analysis.

OBD II · Advanced

Plug-in tracking with vehicle diagnostics

The GX-502 plugs straight into the OBD II port, so a fleet can be fitted without wiring or a workshop visit. It reports location over LTE with GSM fallback, reads vehicle data over K-line and CAN bus, and flags harsh driving.

  • LTE and GSM network
  • On-board diagnostics
  • GPS and BDS positioning
  • Driving behaviour analysis
  • Multiple alerts
  • Effortless plug-in installation
