GT-103

Multi-functional 4G wired vehicle tracker, fitted out of sight.

Wired

Hard-wired tracking for vehicles that stay in the fleet

The GT-103 is wired into the vehicle and concealed. It takes a wide 9–90 V input, is sealed to IP66, and pairs with the GF-201 fuel sensor for location and fuel level from one installation.

  • GPS, BDS and LBS positioning
  • Supports an external buzzer/horn
  • Low-voltage alert
  • Multiple alerts
  • Remote listening
  • IP66 dust and water resistance
